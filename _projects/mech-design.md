Throughout the duration of my Mechanical Design class (MAE 2250) we were tasked with designing and optimizing different devices. 

## **Orange Crusher Optimization**
![orange crusher]({{ "/assets/images/orange-crusher.png" | relative_url }}){: style="width: 550px;"}

This CAD model of an orange crusher was optimized based on testing the force required to crush half of an orange without exceeding the material constraints. I first had to calculate the force that would need to be applied at the top of the crushing surface, then had to work backwards to design an appropriately long lever. All of this had to be balanced with calculating the force at each joint, ensuring that it would not break the acrylic model.
